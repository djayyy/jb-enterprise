## End-to-end visitor journey
1. Header: source navigation (Home, About, Services, Equipment Supply, Contact), phone/email strip and five social icons. LinkedIn destination is supplied in legacy S4; X/Twitter, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram are pictured in S1 but no destination URLs were found. Show clearly pending controls, never guessed accounts or # links.
2. Home: three selectable image slides matching S1 pp.1–3. First title explicitly says Turbine, Generator, Boiler. Other titles are OEM & Non-OEM Turbine Generator Expertise and Retrofitting & Modernization Engineering. Previous/next, labelled selectors and optional play/pause; manual by default, pause for focus/hover/hidden tab, respect reduced motion.
3. Welcome to JB Advanced Engineering, complete source introduction and Why JBAEL (p.4); four image-led industries (p.5); all ten core capabilities (pp.6–7), including visible turbine/generator/boiler choices; certificate image cards (p.8) and source contact prompt (p.9).
4. About: Who We Are, source approach, six What We Do areas, Vision/Mission/Goal, full unattributed Leadership Message (pp.10–12). Do not invent a CEO name/photo.
5. Services: primary Thermal/Hydro photo choices matching p.13, then subservices in an in-page browser. Specialist family follows from pp.24–26/33–34. Switching family/service updates the visible panel without navigation, forced scrolling or page-top reset. Direct detail URLs remain available. Deep links select a family/service; browser state retains no personal data.
6. Equipment: source category catalogue -> contextual quote preview. Detail sidebar links target service content instead of resetting to the top hero. No database/live processing introduced.
7. Certifications: full source certificate images, accessible enlarged view, download-original-image links and standard descriptions from pp.41–43. Source copies shown for design review; issuer verification still a release task.
8. Contact/quote/careers retain honest disconnected status. Deployment, actual uploads/email and policies remain separate backlog tasks.
